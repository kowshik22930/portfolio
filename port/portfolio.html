Projects

PostTrack

A mobile-first web app for postal delivery workers to log parcel handovers.

The problem

Postal workers usually record handovers on paper. It is slow, error-prone and impossible to search. When a delivery is disputed (missed delivery, wrong address, lost parcel), there is no reliable record to check.

The solution

A lightweight tool that works from any phone browser. A worker logs a handover in seconds, and the app saves proof of delivery with GPS coordinates.

What it records

  • Customer details
  • GPS coordinates of the handover
  • Delivery timestamp

The result

Paper logs become a fast, searchable digital record that can settle disputes.
